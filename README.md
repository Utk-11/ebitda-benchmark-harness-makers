# EBITDA Benchmark: Listed Wiring-Harness Makers (FY25 to FY26)

Why does Dhoot Transmission earn a higher EBITDA margin than its listed peers, and where could it still improve? This project benchmarks Dhoot against Motherson Sumi Wiring India (MSWIL) and Minda Corporation, using their published financials.

## The answer

Dhoot's FY26 EBITDA margin of **15.71%** is **5.26 points above the peer median** (10.45%). Almost all of that lead comes from people costs: **8.3% of revenue, against 16.1%** for the peers. But Dhoot spends more than the median on materials (+0.90 points) and other expenses (+1.72 points). Matching the median on those two lines would add about **₹119 crore of EBITDA (2.62 points)**, and every 1% saved on materials is worth **₹29.9 crore (66 basis points)**.

| FY26 | Dhoot | MSWIL | Minda Corp | Peer median |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue from operations (₹ crore) | 4,525 | 11,478 | 6,185 | |
| Revenue growth vs FY25 | 31.4% | 23.1% | 22.3% | |
| Materials, % of revenue | 66.1% | 67.5% | 63.0% | 65.2% |
| Employee costs, % of revenue | 8.3% | 17.2% | 15.1% | 16.1% |
| Other expenses, % of revenue | 9.9% | 6.1% | 10.2% | 8.2% |
| **EBITDA margin** | **15.71%** | 9.24% | 11.66% | 10.45% |

## What changed from FY25 to FY26

Dhoot's margin fell 1.44 points while revenue grew 31.4%. Materials went from 64.8% to 66.1% of revenue and explain most of the fall.

| Step | Margin points |
| --- | --- |
| FY25 EBITDA margin | 17.15 |
| Materials | −1.31 |
| Employee costs | +0.32 |
| Other expenses | −0.45 |
| **FY26 EBITDA margin** | **15.71** |

## Approach

1. Took each company's P&L for FY25 and FY26 from its filings, in ₹ crore.
2. Built a common-size P&L, with every cost line as a % of revenue, so companies of different sizes compare fairly.
3. Benchmarked Dhoot against the peer median line by line, and turned each gap into ₹ crore at Dhoot's FY26 revenue.
4. Built a margin bridge from FY25 to FY26.
5. Sized what a 1% saving on materials is worth.

## Definitions

- **EBITDA** = revenue from operations − materials − employee costs − other expenses. Other income is left out, so these figures can differ from company-reported EBITDA.
- **Materials** = cost of materials consumed + purchases of stock-in-trade + change in inventories.
- Dhoot's other expenses include its impairment loss on financial assets.

## Limits

- The peers differ in product mix and customers. Minda Corporation also makes systems other than wiring harnesses.
- Makers book contract labour in different lines. Taking people and other costs together, Dhoot is at 18.1% of revenue against a 24.3% median.
- An outside-in view can't see contracts, copper pass-through terms or one-off items.
- This is an operating benchmark, not investment advice.
